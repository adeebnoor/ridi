Principal investigator

Adeeb Noor

Professor of Information Technology · computational decision-systems researcher · AI & R&D strategist.

Adeeb Noor is a professor at King Abdulaziz University and serves as Chief Strategy Advisor in the Office of the VP for Research & Innovation. His work spans computational drug safety, semantic technologies, biomedical knowledge integration, interpretable AI, digital health, decision-system evaluation and translational R&D.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science (Bioinformatics) from the University of Colorado Boulder. His doctoral D3 Pharmacovigilance work combined statistical inference, big-data mining and Semantic Web technologies for drug–drug interaction characterization. That research thread now extends through mechanism-aware AI to allocation identity.

His broader professional record includes national-scale education data and workforce planning, hospital digital modernization, research and innovation strategy, and venture-building activity across healthcare and education.
