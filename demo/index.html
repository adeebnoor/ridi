What did the experiment prove?

A high global rank correlation is compatible with any amount of finite decision turnover. Increasing the candidate universe drives ρ toward one without restoring a single displaced top-k identity.

What is RIDI?

RIDI is Jaccard distance applied to finite decision identities: zero means the same candidates receive the slots; one means none do. Changed slots are reported beside it because they map directly to consumed decision capacity.

What does it not prove?

Turnover is not automatically harmful. A new representation may surface better candidates. RIDI measures the change; mechanism-matched controls attribute it, independent outcomes test it, and an exact selector constrains only the avoidable remainder.
