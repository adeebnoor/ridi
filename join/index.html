NDI Lab welcomes rigorous research conversations around computational decision systems, biomedical AI, reproducibility, allocation-aware evaluation and translational R&D.

Students

Prospective researchers

Research collaborators

Applied R&D

Industry & government

Best fit: real decision settings where scientific methods, domain knowledge and operational constraints can produce publishable and useful work.

How to approach

A strong first message is specific.

Name the research question or programme that interests you.
Describe the dataset, system, domain or technical problem you can bring.
State what would count as a useful result, including a negative or falsifying result.
Link code, a paper, a short proposal or evidence of prior work when available.
