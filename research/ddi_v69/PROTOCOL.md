# RIDI-DDI-v69: exploratory transport protocol

Frozen before inspecting allocation outcomes in this extension, 14 September 2026. This is an author-side exploratory analysis lock, NOT an independent preregistration or a confirmatory clinical study. Related DDI resources and cohorts have been analysed previously for different questions. Results, nulls and failures must all be retained.

## Question and data
Does an archived DDInter knowledge-base expansion change allocations made by a fixed research burden-ranking rule, on a fixed candidate universe, and what does a minimum-replacement selector retain? This is not a deployed clinical alert system, a safety test or evidence of patient harm.

## Units and mapping
A. Catalogue-level feasibility: the original DDInter unordered non-self pairs are the common candidate universe. Primary k=1000; report k=100,500,1000,5000 regardless of outcomes.
B. Reconstructed potential-alert allocation, when source access permits: NHANES 2015-2016 and 2017-2018 reported prescription ingredients in the previous month. Split semicolon-delimited combination products explicitly; never map a multiword drug by its first token. Use exact, collision-checked normalized matching. No unvalidated salt stripping or guessed synonym map. Report mapped and unmapped rows, ingredients, participants, and pair counts. Candidate pairs must be in the original DDInter graph so universe expansion cannot explain turnover. This is reported use in a month, not verified simultaneous ingestion or a recorded alert episode. Primary k=1 per participant; report k=3,5. Primary estimand is over participants with more candidates than k; also report all participants with any eligible candidate, including saturated queues. These capacities are research conventions, NOT clinical recommendations. Analyse the cohorts separately and pooled, unweighted; make no US population-prevalence claim.
C. MIMIC-IV Demo v2.2 is optional transport only. Require documented overlapping prescription intervals, not simply the same admission. Do not use admission-based co-occurrence as a substitute. No patient identifiers or patient-level derivatives are to be committed or included in the final shareable package.

## Fixed rankers and endpoints
Primary score for pair (a,b) in graph v: degree_v(a)+degree_v(b). Sensitivity: degree_v(a)*degree_v(b). These are workload/connectivity proxies, not severity, probability or clinical utility. Use descending score with a fixed canonical lexicographic pair tie-break. Keep graph pairs common across versions and record graph expansion separately. Do not select another ranker because results disappoint.

For each prespecified capacity report changed slots, changed-slot fraction and Jaccard distance/RIDI, selected-score utility, exact tie prevalence at the cutoff and an unchanged-pipeline/input-row-permutation replay. Count-preserving or score-matched artificial constructions, if ever added, must be separated from this natural knowledge-update analysis.

Run the exact minimum-replacement selector at eta={0,0.0001,0.001,0.005,0.01}; eta=0.001 is the primary descriptive control setting, not a clinical non-inferiority margin. Optimize raw updated burden-score sum. Report both ratio-of-total replacement reduction and mean per-unit avoidable fraction over units with turnover, identifying their denominators. They are not interchangeable. Do not use outcome labels to select a ranker, tune eta or choose k.

External priority annotations, when original source files are accessible, are the fixed ONC high-priority and explicitly non-interruptive lists. Absence from either list is UNKNOWN, not a safe or true-negative label. Exclude conflicting labels from labelled contrasts and report them. These are externally sourced annotations, not proven independent of all upstream curation and not observed adverse outcomes. Report high-priority alerts retained, dropped and added and the actual identities in a controlled audit trail, not just equal counts. Any sparse or empty endpoint stays sparse or empty; zero observed losses does not establish safety. No AUROC using unknown=negative.

## Uncertainty, safeguards and reporting
Catalogue results are descriptive for a fixed graph: do not manufacture independent sample sizes from dependent pairs. For NHANES, use a paired participant bootstrap, 10,000 draws, seed 20260914, with cycle stratification for the pooled estimate. This describes empirical sample variation, not the complex survey design or source/mapping uncertainty. Report all capacities and eta values; no selected p-values or clinical equivalence claims. Null results remain in the report. Clinical effectiveness and harm require a different, authorised outcome study.

Code must fail visibly for missing/corrupt sources, hashes or mappings. Synthetic fixtures are only unit tests. Public reports contain aggregates, source URLs and hashes, and reconstruction code. Third-party pair-level inputs and patient-level derivatives are not included in the final public package. No Nature submission, preprint publication or CODECHECK certification is implied by this branch.
