Research

From knowledge to action.

NDI Lab studies the informational path by which computational systems move from evidence to inference, from inference to explanation, and from explanation to real-world action.

Three questions

The research programme

Knowledge

What does the system know?

Representation, Semantic Web, ontologies, biomedical knowledge integration and evidence structure.

Explanation

Why does it make this prediction?

Mechanistic inference, computational drug safety, interpretable AI and ontology-enhanced learning.

Action

Who ultimately receives the action?

Capacity-limited decision systems, allocation identity, reproducibility and governance at the score-to-action boundary.

Current programmes

Research streams

Allocation Identity & RIDICurrent flagship. Studies whether conventional evaluation identifies which entities receive finite action and how allocations change across model, data or representation updates.
Mechanistic & Interpretable Biomedical AIExtends drug-safety research from statistical association toward biological mechanism, ontology-informed representation and interpretable prediction.
Knowledge-to-Decision SystemsStudies how representation, inference, explanation, validation and governance combine in systems deployed in healthcare, education and public-sector decision environments.
Research philosophy

Positive results are not the only useful results.

The programme values independent replication, counterexamples, boundary cases, prospective tests and negative findings that clarify when a proposed phenomenon matters and when it does not.
