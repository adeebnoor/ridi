# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: participate respectfully, critique claims and code rather than people, disclose conflicts, preserve scientific uncertainty, and protect confidential information.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, fabricated evidence and attempts to expose private or restricted data are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the institutional contact listed on the author's ORCID profile. Do not place sensitive details in a public issue.
