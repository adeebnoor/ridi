RIDI

Same audit. Different identities.

RIDI studies allocation identity in capacity-limited AI: when scores become a finite queue, shortlist or top-k action, which entities actually receive action — and which identities change?

Try the 60-second experimentResearch guideCode & reproducibility
The scientific object

Allocation identity at the score-to-action boundary

Performance, fairness, calibration and robustness remain necessary evaluation dimensions. RIDI asks a different question: whether the identities receiving finite action are identified and stable under the comparison of interest.

Measure

Who changed?

Compare equal-capacity selected sets directly rather than infer membership stability from rank correlation alone.

Test

What caused the change?

Use matched controls, representation interventions, model updates and prospective designs to distinguish mechanisms.

Report

What should be visible?

Report capacity, selection rule, comparator, identity turnover, conventional audit results and outcome gates together.

Evidence programme

Positive results, controls and failures

COMPASConstructive audit-equivalent allocations show that matched aggregate summaries can leave individual identities unresolved.
EPSSProduction-version changes provide a cybersecurity setting for top-k identity turnover with controls and prospective follow-up.
CMS HVBPA second production scoring system tests transport beyond cybersecurity.
Controlled mechanismsRepresentation and model interventions test how finite allocations can move when conventional summaries move little.
Registered failuresRxNorm and Open Targets analyses are retained to delimit empirical prevalence and avoid turning the theorem into a universal turnover claim.
