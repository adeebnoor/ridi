From knowledge
to action.

We build and study computational systems that must represent, infer, explain, decide and translate — reliably enough to matter in the real world.

The programme connects biomedical informatics, semantic technologies, interpretable AI, decision-system evaluation, and translational R&D. Its central question is simple: what information is required for a computational decision to be scientifically trustworthy and operationally useful?

Current flagship research · RIDI

Same audit. Different identities.

A system can pass the same reported aggregate audit while acting on different entities.

RIDI extends the programme from prediction and explanation to the score-to-action boundary. It studies allocation identity: when a model or ranking has finite capacity, who actually receives the action — and who changed?

Founder & research lead

Adeeb Noor

Professor of Information Technology · AI & R&D strategist · computational decision-systems researcher.

His work spans government, higher education and healthcare, combining scientific research with institutional strategy and product translation. The CV describes national-level programme oversight, AI and governance modernization, venture building, and large-scale digital platforms.

At King Abdulaziz University he serves as Chief Strategy Advisor in the Office of the VP for Research & Innovation, alongside his academic role. Earlier roles include senior advisory work in the Saudi Ministry of Education, executive AI strategy, hospital digital modernization, and collaborative R&D at KAUST.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science (Bioinformatics) from the University of Colorado Boulder. His doctoral D3 Pharmacovigilance work combined big-data mining and Semantic Web technologies for drug–drug interaction characterization; the same intellectual thread now extends through mechanism-aware AI to allocation identity.
