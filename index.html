02 / HOW I WORK

From idea
to operating system.

01

Find the missing variable.

Look for what the dominant metric or framework leaves invisible—identity, context, coverage, implementation or behavioral consequence.

02

Make the claim falsifiable.

Convert the intuition into a testable design with explicit controls, failure modes, preregistration or reproducible evidence where possible.

03

Build the thing.

Turn the argument into software, an interactive demonstration, a governance mechanism or an instructional system that others can inspect.

Try the flagship result.

03 / TRAJECTORY

Research shaped
by real systems.

Teaching, institutional work and software building are not side projects; they are where the research questions become visible.

COMPUTING EDUCATION

Designing for readiness, not content coverage.

Course and curriculum work evolved from lecture design into structured systems for evidence, alignment, cultural context and measurable student readiness.

HEALTH KNOWLEDGE

Studying what knowledge bases leave out.

Drug-interaction work focuses on completeness, popularity, mechanism and the consequences of heterogeneous biomedical knowledge.

DECISION INTELLIGENCE

Moving from performance to allocation.

RIDI extends the question from “how accurate is the system?” to “who or what occupies the finite action set, and did that identity change?”

INSTITUTIONS

Making governance computationally legible.

Current work applies the same discipline to research merit, partnerships, academic systems and the design of decision processes inside institutions.

04 / POSITION

Good evaluation tells us how a system performs. Good decision intelligence also asks what the system actually allocates, exposes and changes.
