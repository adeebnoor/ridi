We study how computational systems represent evidence, make inferences, explain predictions, and ultimately decide who receives action.

NDI Lab is the research-program identity led by Adeeb Noor. The work connects biomedical informatics, semantic technologies, mechanistic and interpretable AI, and the scientific evaluation of decision systems — with a parallel track translating research into deployed health, education and government systems.

Current flagship paper · RIDI

Identical audits, different AI decisions

A finite AI system can satisfy exactly the same reported audit while acting differently because the selected identities differ.

In the preregistered RAG test, the complete relevance-grade-by-position vector and all registered retrieval metrics were held exactly fixed while only metric-zero passage identities changed. The equal-dataset-weight macro correctness-flip rate was 17.27%; an order-only control was 4.8%.
