Measure what changed.
Budget what had to change.

RIDI measures how much a ranking update changes the selected items, then computes the exact minimum turnover compatible with a declared rank-utility budget. The goal is not to freeze old decisions; it is to make update-induced churn explicit, measurable and auditable.

Constructive contribution

The identity–utility frontier

For a baseline top-k set and updated scores, RIDI evaluates the best updated-score utility attainable at every replacement count. The exact solution with j outsiders retains the highest-ranked incumbents and adds the highest-ranked outsiders; sorted prefix sums recover the full frontier in O(n log n) time.

The frontier answers a practical question: given the scoring objective we have chosen, what is the smallest selection change that keeps us within an explicit utility-loss tolerance?

No retraining and no training data are required. The result is conditional on the declared objective and tolerance; it is not a claim that keeping old selections is always desirable.

Why selected identity deserves its own audit

The supporting experiments separate evaluation equality, selected membership and downstream outcomes rather than treating them as interchangeable.

Preregistered RAG17.27% benchmark-defined correctness changes with the complete assessed grade sequence and five retrieval metrics exactly unchanged.Exact equality in this audit does not identify the evidence or certify behavioral equivalence.

Important: RAG correctness changes were bidirectional (60 correct→incorrect, 74 incorrect→correct). EPSS outcomes are sparse and retrospective. Exploratory drug-interaction analyses are not clinical validation. RIDI does not turn identity stability into a safety, fairness or benefit claim.
Reproducibility

What has — and has not — been independently checked

Checked

The sealed EPSS numerical workflow was reproduced by two external executors. Both also performed targeted blind regeneration of the illustrative SciFact case in distinct environments. Registered RAG code, inputs and analysis are frozen through the OSF record.

Boundaries

The targeted SciFact runs did not both reproduce a strict-scored correctness change because one pinned-model reference began with Verdict: and was rejected by the frozen parser. A post hoc 300-query parser sensitivity is reported separately. No longer-generation sensitivity has yet been completed.

CODECHECK: no formal certificate has been issued. Register issue #208 was closed with an invitation to return once a public preprint is available or the manuscript is under journal review.
